# Morrow Studio — concept product art direction

Created for the 30 September 2026 Happy Mondays review revision. Morrow Studio, Form Studio, Sunday Movement, Aster Studio and Tempo Studio are illustrative concept brands here; products, prices and ratings do not represent actual client results or inventory. The page identifies the concept brand and illustrative figures beneath the two scenes.

## Art direction

Follow the user-endorsed warm olive/travertine composition, rather than converting it to an opaque catalogue panel. Keep real photographic texture, an oversized isolated product, native translucent interfaces and foreground detail. White surroundings; burgundy, sage, stone and cream provide natural colour beyond the blue hero.

## Assets

- `courtyard.webp`: clean olive courtyard/travertine background plate, built-in ImageGen. Magnific Precision Photo 2× (zero creative reinterpretation; sharpness 5, grain 5, detail 4) produced a 2508px master. Website variant is 1920px, quality 88 WebP.
- `studio.webp`: separate warm plaster, linen and rounded travertine photographic stage, built-in ImageGen, 1254px, quality 92 WebP.
- `grip-sock.webp`: transparent cream/burgundy knitwear foreground, built-in ImageGen, 1254px square. Original alpha retained, quality 92 WebP. Shared lighting and coherent product across the two settings.
- `product-0.webp` through `product-3.webp`: separate transparent catalogue cutouts in cream/burgundy, sage, charcoal and cream/navy. Built-in ImageGen; 900px tall, quality 90 WebP, full alpha. Individual assets avoid fragments from neighbouring products in the initial contact sheet.

The Google mark and all search, product, basket and confirmation typography remain native SVG/HTML/CSS. No text-bearing generated mockup is shipped as the finished interface.
