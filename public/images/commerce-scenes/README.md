## Generated material

Built-in imagegen created `light-field.webp`. It depicts defocused light only. No generated products, logos, interfaces or people are used in the rendered continuation. Original generated PNG retained under the Codex generated_images directory, converted to WebP quality 92 at its native 1254×1254 resolution. CSS changes the light treatment for the second section.

Prompt:
> Generate a high resolution photographic light field texture to serve as the backdrop for a luxury website product animation. Square image. Extremely defocused natural scene, no legible objects, no UI, no product, no text, no hard edges. Soft olive/sage green shadows primarily lower left, luminous silvery sage glow upper right, warm limestone cream drifting through centre. Deep elegant tonal range, analog large-format lens defocus, incredibly smooth light falloff with imperceptibly fine photographic grain. Rich darker muted green-brown at bottom and bright diffuse pale glow at top. It should evoke looking through heavily frosted glass into a sunlit Mediterranean courtyard, not look like a basic computer gradient. Refined Apple product marketing material. The scene is soft and blurry but the file must be clean high-resolution, no banding. No bokeh circles, no orange, no teal, no purple, no neon.

A separate generated full-section mockup was used for art-direction exploration only. Its generated sock, invented reviews and frosted Google UI were not shipped. The mockup is saved in the workspace output/happy-mondays-rebuild-2026-09-30/composition-study.png.
