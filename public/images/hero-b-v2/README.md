# Concept B — photographic terrace

Built-in ImageGen created the landscape composition and a separate portrait composition. Magnific upscaled the selected desktop image 4× (450 existing credits) and portrait 2× (180 credits). No credit purchase. These are AI-created scenes, not photographs of a verified location.

Desktop native: 1672 × 940. Verified master: 6688 × 3760. Mobile native: 1122 × 1402. Verified master: 2240 × 2800. Masters and originals are archived locally under `.design/hero-b-v2/` and excluded from the deployment. Responsive AVIF/WebP exports are served from this folder. Desktop widths: 1440, 2400, 3840. Mobile: 800, 1200, 1600. Native HTML/SVG carries all interface content.

Desktop generation prompt (built-in tool):

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: high resolution full-bleed website hero photograph, landscape 16:9. Create an exceptionally refined architectural editorial photograph from a whitewashed Cycladic terrace overlooking the Aegean Sea. A calm expansive blue sea fills the left 65 percent, with a straight horizon around the upper third, soft hazy distant island low on the far left horizon. At right a graceful curving white lime-plaster parapet and a single sculptural white arch partially cropped at far right, terrace steps leading down toward the sea. Lower right sunlit white plaster surface, believable fine tactile grain, subtle broad cool shadow. The architecture occupies rightmost 30 percent and bottom 15 percent only. Left-middle open rich medium-dark azure water is clean negative space for white webpage typography that will be added in code; NO words or UI in image. Natural bright morning Mediterranean sunlight, sophisticated quiet optimism, precise premium architecture photography on medium format camera, realistic water ripples with fine variations, gently atmospheric distance, no HDR. Carefully controlled palette of chalk white, azure, muted blue sky. Wide considered composition with a beautiful but understated architectural focal point; photographic not rendered. No railings, ropes, umbrellas, furniture, people, pool, plants, boats, town, logos, oversaturated turquoise, artificial blur, sunset or cartoon clouds. Output at highest available resolution, 3840x2160 preferred.

Portrait edit prompt (built-in tool; original desktop image as reference):

Create the matching portrait mobile art direction for this same premium Cycladic photograph. Portrait 4:5 composition, highest resolution. Keep exactly the same real photographic morning light, rich blue Aegean, subtle distant island, finely textured white lime-plaster curved parapet and architectural arch. Recompose rather than squeezing the landscape: sky upper 20 percent, uninterrupted medium-dark blue water from 25 to 78 percent on LEFT and CENTER; arch becomes a thin graceful element confined to rightmost 15 percent, rising through the upper half. Bottom 15 percent is softly sunlit white curved parapet with cool gentle shadows. Need clear dark blue water on left/center for white webpage headline and body copy placed later in code. The left 75 percent of frame must be free of architecture until the bottom. Keep natural details and optical realism. No text, UI, people, furniture, plants or added objects.

The browser deliberately crops the portrait to the narrower phone frame; sea and curved plaster remain visible. This is a separate composition, not a stretched landscape. Export resizing/encoding uses Lanczos, desktop AVIF 70 / WebP 88, mobile AVIF 65 / WebP 85. Upscaling increases deliverable dimensions; it does not make these native camera photographs.
