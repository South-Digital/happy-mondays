# Product photography — Concept A continuation

30 September 2026. AI-created concept products, not photographs of a client's catalogue and not a claim that these brands are clients.

## Production

- Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool; native output for each: 1,122 × 1,402 PNG.
- Magnific Precision Photo, 2×, sharpness 5 / grain 4 / detail 5. 180 existing credits each, 360 total.
- Upscaled masters: 2,244 × 2,804 JPEG. Visually inspected at full-detail crops.
- Responsive widths 640 / 1,122 / 1,600 / 2,240 in AVIF (75) and WebP (88); Lanczos downsampling only.
- JPEG working masters and originals remain in `.design/product-studies/`, outside the public build.
- Site assets: `public/images/product-studies/jewellery-*.{avif,webp}` and `ritual-*.{avif,webp}`.
- Image crops and detail zoom are CSS presentation of the same source. The 2× masters are upscaled, not native 2K captures.
- Product RITUAL is an illustrative packaging concept; no endorsement or real client relationship is implied.

## Final generation prompts

### Jewellery

Use case: product-mockup. Create a high-end editorial product photograph for a premium ecommerce agency website. Asset is a portrait 4:5 image, 2048px if supported. TWO matching sculptural gold hoop earrings, believable 18k polished gold, organically rounded slightly chunky tapered oval hoops with realistic tiny fastening detail. Beautiful physical product, no gemstones. The pair rests naturally at different angles on a softly folded ivory silk fabric in the lower central portion of the composition. Very close considered still-life photography, not a floating 3D render. The hoops should occupy about 45% of image width, centered at x52%, y52%. Foreground silk has tactile weave and gentle sculptural folds; background recedes to luminous warm ivory and soft out-of-focus olive foliage light, no identifiable plants in foreground. Gold retains detailed highlights with elegant dark reflections, no overexposure. Directional diffused morning window light from upper left, very soft natural contact shadows, shallow but not excessive depth of field. Product pin sharp and both hoops fully resolved. Restrained palette champagne gold, creamy white, desaturated olive. Quiet negative space in top 22% and bottom 22%, room for native website UI overlays. Premium fashion campaign, luxury jewellery art direction, medium-format photographic realism. No text, no logos, no boxes, no hands, no graphics, no borders, no collage, no watermark.

### Beauty

Use case: product-mockup. One premium editorial beauty product photograph, portrait 4:5 composition, 2048px if supported. Art direction for a luxury ecommerce studio, believable medium-format photograph, exceptional detail. A single softly rounded translucent smoky-amber glass face-oil bottle, cylindrical 30ml bottle with thick clear amber base, ivory rounded cylindrical cap. A very small cream label with only the simple word RITUAL in restrained dark-grey sans serif; no other writing. Bottle stands upright on warm ivory limestone, positioned x53%, y53%, occupies 47% of frame height and about 27% of width. Its physical materials are the subject: glow of warm light through amber glass, subtle real reflections, gently textured matte cap, delicate condensation-free glass, beautiful contact shadow. Behind it out of focus: a cream plaster wall with broad soft bands of morning sun and diffuse shadow, a hint of muted green plant light at upper left, warm ivory to pale apricot ambience, no flowers or props. The composition feels luminous, tactile and expensive, not a sterile ecommerce cutout or CGI render. Bottle is perfectly crisp, stone has fine texture; background is a soft naturally blurred field. Keep top 20% and bottom 22% low-detail so native web UI can overlay later. No additional products, no hands, no people, no typography other than RITUAL, no graphic overlays, no border, no watermark.
